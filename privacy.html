Beeframe Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Data Beeframe accesses

Beeframe connects to Google only after you sign in, grant permission, and choose a spreadsheet. It reads and writes that selected spreadsheet to provide the apiary-management features you request.

Google authorization

Beeframe requests only the Google Drive drive.file scope, which is limited to files opened or created with the app. It does not request your email address or profile. Google Sheets sharing settings continue to control who else can access the spreadsheet.

Storage and sharing

Beeframe is a static browser application. It has no Beeframe server, database, service account, or client secret. Google access tokens stay in browser memory and are lost on refresh. Spreadsheet data is processed in your browser and stored in the Google Sheet you selected; Beeframe does not copy it to an application database.

External services

The browser contacts Google for authentication, Picker, and Sheets. Like ordinary web services, Google may receive network information such as your IP address and browser details. Beeframe does not request your device's location.

Questions

For privacy questions, open an issue in the project repository.
